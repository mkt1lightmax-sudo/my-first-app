Privacy Policy

LIGHTMAX Inbox · LIGHTMAX (THAILAND) CO.,LTD · Last updated 18 November 2026

LIGHTMAX Inbox is the internal customer-service application of LIGHTMAX (THAILAND) CO.,LTD ("we", "us"), a solar installation company in Thailand. Our staff use it to read and reply to messages that customers send to our own LINE Official Accounts and Facebook Pages. This policy explains what data the application handles and why.

1. Data we collect

  • From people who message our LINE Official Accounts or Facebook Pages: the platform-provided user ID (for example a Facebook Page-scoped ID or LINE user ID), display name, profile picture, the messages you send us (text, images, stickers, files, locations) and the time they were sent.
  • Information you choose to give us in the chat, which our staff may note to serve you: phone number, LINE ID, installation address, and notes about your request.
  • Staff accounts: name, work email and role of our employees who use the application.

2. How we use it

  • To show your messages to our staff and to send you the replies they write.
  • To follow up on your enquiry, quotation, site survey or installation.
  • To keep internal records of sales handled through chat.

We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not send messages to people who have not contacted us first. Replies are written by our staff, not sent automatically.

3. Where it is stored and who receives it

Data is stored in our database hosted by Neon and served through Vercel. Messages are exchanged through LINE (LY Corporation) and Meta Platforms (Facebook Messenger / Instagram) under their own terms. Only our authorised staff can access the application, and each staff role sees only what it needs.

4. How long we keep it

We keep conversation data for as long as needed to serve you and for our business records, normally no longer than 3 years after your last message, unless the law requires longer.

5. Your rights

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) you may ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its use. To delete your data, follow the steps on our Data Deletion Instructions page.
